ABOUT

A clinical perspective, an engineering mindset.

I am a 500-level medical student at the University of Ibadan and an AI/ML engineer focused on building and studying intelligent systems for healthcare.

BASED INIbadan, NigeriaWORKING ACROSSMedicine · Imaging · AI
01 / PROFILE

Turning clinical problems into questions that can be investigated, modelled and tested.

My training in medicine has shaped the way I approach technology. A model is not useful simply because it performs well on a benchmark. It must also be considered in the context of the patients, clinical workflows, data quality and infrastructure in which it may eventually be used.

My current research centres on 3D brain tumour segmentation using African imaging datasets, with particular interest in Mamba-based state-space models, Vision Transformers, domain adaptation and compute efficiency. I am also involved in cardiovascular risk prediction research and the development of structured African clinical datasets.

Alongside research, I build applied AI systems, including retrieval-augmented assistants and healthcare-focused machine-learning applications. I enjoy translating technical ideas into practical projects and helping other learners develop the skills to contribute to this field.
